Requirements

macOS 13 or later and about 1.5 GB of free disk space.

Play

After installation, open Applications and double-click Plants vs. Zombies Hybrid v0.25 English.

Runtime

The game includes its own Godot and .NET runtimes and does not require CrossOver or a separate .NET installation. The Intel game core runs through Rosetta 2 on Apple silicon.

Saves

English saves are kept under your user Library, separately from the Chinese release. Reinstalling the app does not delete them.
