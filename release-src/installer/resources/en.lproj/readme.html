Requirements

macOS 13 or later and about 1.5 GB of free disk space.

Play

After installation, open Applications and double-click PVZ Hybrid v0.25.

Runtime

The game includes its own Godot and .NET runtimes and does not require CrossOver. The Intel game core runs through Rosetta 2 on Apple silicon.

Saves

Saves are kept under your user Library. Reinstalling the app does not delete them.
